Faredict — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Introduction

Faredict (formerly Driver Companion) (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a companion app for professional ride-hailing drivers, available on Android and iPhone. It shows on-device visual indicators based on information visible on your screen, and offers optional on-device GPS mileage tracking and shift records. On iPhone, the live reading feature is called Faredict Radar.

We handle personal data in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Please read this policy before using the App.

2. Information We Collect

Faredict does not collect any data, on Android or on iPhone.

All processing happens on your device. The App has no servers, and we receive nothing from it. We do not collect, transmit or share:

  • Ride request details, fares, distances or times
  • Pickup or drop-off addresses
  • Passenger names, numbers or any rider data
  • Your earnings, mileage or shift records
  • Your location or GPS coordinates
  • Screenshots or screen recordings
  • Keystrokes, messages, passwords or content from other apps
  • Device identifiers, analytics or crash reports

3. How the App Reads Your Screen

Android

When a supported ride-hailing app is in the foreground, the App takes a momentary screenshot of the screen, reads the text on it on your device (OCR), calculates its indicators, and then discards the image. Screenshots are held in memory only. They are never saved, uploaded or shared. The App only acts on supported ride-hailing apps. In split-screen mode, or during a quick app switch, the screenshot includes whatever else is visible on screen, such as a navigation app, and the on-device text reading (OCR) runs over that whole image. Only trip offer details from supported ride-hailing apps are used. The image and the text read from it are held in memory only and are discarded straight away. Nothing is stored or sent.

iPhone (iOS)

Each shift, you start Apple’s screen broadcast (ReplayKit) by tapping Start Broadcast in Apple’s own sheet. While it runs, iPhone shows its red recording indicator. The broadcast also stops when the phone locks.

A broadcast extension reads only the lower part of the screen, on your device, using Apple’s on-device text recognition. Screen images (frames) are held in memory only. They are never saved and never uploaded. The extension makes no network calls.

The latest confirmed offer result (fare and times) is saved in the App’s private shared storage (an App Group) so the App can show it. It is cleared when the broadcast starts and when it finishes.

The verdict is shown in the Dynamic Island / Live Activity and on the Lock Screen. The Dynamic Island exists only on iPhone 14 Pro and newer.

Optional Apple Shortcuts actions (Check Uber Offer, Check Offer (Text Only) and Latest Offer Verdict) are also available. They work on your device only.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

Android

Shift earnings, mileage, GPS-derived distance, notes, trip history, targets and your settings are stored only in the App’s private storage on your device. On Android, cloud and device backup of this data is switched off. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, it is permanently deleted and we cannot recover it.

Test versions of the App may keep an optional diagnostic log (off by default) with redacted trip details, stored on your device only. The standard release version from 2.0.1 onwards does not write a diagnostic log. Nothing is ever sent to us.

iPhone (iOS)

Shift records, earnings, mileage, targets and your settings are stored only in the App’s private storage on your device. If you delete the App, they are deleted with it, and we cannot recover them. On iPhone, the on-device log and shift history stay on the phone and may be included in your own iPhone or iCloud backup if you use one. We never receive it.

The App keeps a diagnostic log on your device only. Postcodes and street names are removed from it, and it is capped at 2 MB. It never leaves your phone, except in your own backup (see above) or when you tap Share log. You can choose to share it yourself (More → About → Share log), or clear it.

5. Permissions Used

Android

Accessibility Service — used only to detect when a supported ride-hailing app shows a trip card, and to read the text on it so the App can show its indicators. While a supported ride-hailing app is in split screen, it also receives screen-change notifications (not their content) from the Waze navigation app, only so the App knows when to take its next reading. This stops when split screen ends. It does not read passwords or messages, track keystrokes, or control any app.

Display Over Other Apps (Overlay) — used to show a colour-coded screen border, an information card and a small, faint, visible dot. None of these can be tapped and none block touches. The dot has no function other than helping Android keep the App running smoothly while monitoring is on. You can turn it off in the App (More).

Location (GPS) — only if you enable automatic mileage tracking. Location is used on your device to calculate distance driven during an active shift, is never transmitted, and is not kept as a location history. Only the resulting mileage total is stored. While tracking, Android shows a notification. You can use manual mileage entry instead.

Notifications — to show the tracking notification and alerts such as monitoring switching off.

Battery optimisation exemption — optional; helps stop Android from closing the App during a shift.

The App never requests background location access.

iPhone (iOS)

Screen broadcast — started by you each shift in Apple’s own sheet, as described in section 3.

Location — optional. It is used on your device to calculate shift mileage, and to keep the App running in the background during a shift or while the Dynamic Island is showing. The iOS permission can be While Using or Always. Location is never sent anywhere and no location history is kept; only the mileage total is stored.

Notifications — we send a notification when live reading stops, and one when the Dynamic Island (Live Activity) ends.

6. Automatic Switch-Off

On Android, the App may end an active shift automatically after a period with no trips, and switches monitoring off after 3 hours with no trips. The Accessibility Service stays enabled in Android settings until you turn it off.

7. Data Sharing

Because we collect no data, there is nothing to share. We do not share information with ride-hailing platforms, advertisers, analytics providers or any third party. The App has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

8. If You Contact Us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use it only to reply and improve the App, and we delete it when it’s no longer needed.

9. Beta Tester Sign-Up

This section covers the early-access sign-up form only, not the App. If you sign up to test the App through our Google Form, we collect your first name, last name and the Gmail address you use for the Google Play Store. We use them only to add you to the Play Store testing list, send you your invitation, and contact you about testing, including any tester reward. We do not use them for marketing or share them with anyone else.

The form and its responses are held in Google Forms and Google Sheets under our Google account, so Google processes them on our behalf. We keep your details only while the testing programme runs and delete them when it ends, or earlier if you ask us to at faredict@appbuildersstudio.com. Removing your details from our list does not remove you from Google Play’s testing programme: you can leave that yourself from the app’s Play Store page.

This form is for the Android app through the Google Play Store.

10. Children

Faredict is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

11. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights over your personal data. We hold none about you through the App, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you signed up as a beta tester (section 9), you can ask us to access, correct or delete your sign-up details. Everything the App stores is on your device and under your control.

12. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If we ever add a feature that sends data off your device, we will update this policy and the App’s in-app disclosure before it does so.

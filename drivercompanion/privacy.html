Driver Companion — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

1. Introduction

Driver Companion (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a companion app for professional ride-hailing drivers. It shows on-device visual indicators based on information visible on your screen, and offers optional on-device GPS mileage tracking and shift records.

We handle personal data in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Please read this policy before using the App.

2. Information We Collect

Driver Companion does not collect any data.

All processing happens on your device. The App has no servers and makes no network calls. We do not collect, transmit or share:

  • Ride request details, fares, distances or times
  • Pickup or drop-off addresses
  • Passenger names, numbers or any rider data
  • Your earnings, mileage or shift records
  • Your location or GPS coordinates
  • Screenshots or screen recordings
  • Keystrokes, messages, passwords or content from other apps
  • Device identifiers, analytics or crash reports

3. How the App Reads Your Screen

When a supported ride-hailing app is in the foreground, the App takes a momentary screenshot of the screen, reads the text on it on your device (OCR), calculates its indicators, and then discards the image. Screenshots are held in memory only. They are never saved, uploaded or shared. The App is designed to ignore other apps and content.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

Shift earnings, mileage, GPS-derived distance, notes, trip history, targets and your settings are stored only in the App’s private storage on your device. Cloud and device backup of this data is switched off. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, it is permanently deleted and we cannot recover it.

The App has an optional diagnostic log (off by default). If you switch it on, a text log with redacted trip details is stored on your device only. Nothing is sent to us.

5. Permissions Used

Accessibility Service — used only to detect when a supported ride-hailing app shows a trip card, and to read the text on it so the App can show its indicators. It does not read passwords or messages, track keystrokes, or control any app.

Display Over Other Apps (Overlay) — used to show a colour-coded screen border, an information card and a small, faint, visible dot. None of these can be tapped and none block touches. The dot has no function other than helping Android keep the App running smoothly while monitoring is on. You can turn it off in the App (More).

Location (GPS) — only if you enable automatic mileage tracking. Location is used on your device to calculate distance driven during an active shift, is never transmitted, and is not kept as a location history. Only the resulting mileage total is stored. While tracking, Android shows a notification. You can use manual mileage entry instead.

Notifications — to show the tracking notification and alerts such as monitoring switching off.

Battery optimisation exemption — optional; helps stop Android from closing the App during a shift.

The App never requests background location access.

6. Automatic Switch-Off

If monitoring is on and no trip has been detected for 3 hours, the App switches monitoring off and ends an active shift. The Accessibility Service stays enabled in Android settings until you turn it off.

7. Data Sharing

Because we collect no data, there is nothing to share. We do not share information with ride-hailing platforms, advertisers, analytics providers or any third party. The App has no advertising and no third-party tracking.

8. If You Contact Us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use it only to reply and improve the App, and we delete it when it’s no longer needed.

9. Children

Driver Companion is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

10. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights over your personal data. We hold none about you through the App, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Everything the App stores is on your device and under your control.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If we ever add a feature that sends data off your device, we will update this policy and the App’s in-app disclosure before it does so.
